# SEO content and listing verification queue

Prepared 14 August 2026 from the 346 published Supabase business records available to the Vercel Preview environment. This is a verification backlog, not evidence that any listed claim is currently correct.

## Definition of done for a listing

- Verify the exact outlet name, full address, unit and postcode.
- Verify current phone, official website/menu, seven-day opening hours and map coordinates.
- Record the halal evidence type, source URL, certificate number where applicable, outlet scope, checked date and expiry/recheck date.
- Add original or owner-supplied photos with provenance; do not use unrelated venue imagery.
- Replace generic quality claims with sourced facts or first-hand editorial notes.
- Show the verification date and corrections route on the public page; ensure visible data and Restaurant schema match.

- Rewrite one priority guide per week only after its linked listings meet the definition of done.
- Recheck MUIS evidence at least every 90 days and before any high-traffic seasonal update.
- Keep a visible change log and record corrections within two business days.
