## Serving design
- Current editorial templates use dedicated units without the retired /api/ads API.
- Articles: >=600 body words, >=350 words and two body paragraphs before each ad, >=two sections between ads, maximum three. No ads after the final section or at newsletter/lead/pull-quote breaks. Noindex posts excluded.
- Home: one unit after latest guides. Blog/category lists: one full-width unit after six cards, only when at least nine cards exist.
- Selected tools: one unit after content. Explicit allowlist in lib/editorial-ads.ts.
- ALL Quran pages (index, surahs and search) remain ad-free. Devotional readers, prayer tools, maps, contact/legal/admin and other unlisted surfaces do not receive placements.
- Units reserve 280px plus label/padding before hydration; variable-height Google creatives can still be taller. Measure field CLS after real serving begins.
- Requests begin only with marketing consent and within 300px of the viewport. Zero-width units are deferred. No timed ad refresh. New route = new ad element.
- One deduplicated loader; preview/localhost use data-adtest=on. Google regional CMP handles the additional regional consent flow. The site's custom banner is not a replacement for a certified CMP.
- Consent refusal, unfilled units and loader failures show a first-party guide recommendation in the reserved space. No ads requested before marketing consent. Footer Cookie settings permits withdrawal.
- Existing tracking no longer labels a queued request as an AdSense impression; filled status is observed separately. Google AdSense reporting remains authoritative for billable impressions/revenue.
