## Source and freshness policy

Sensitive claims were written from current primary sources, including the [MUIS halal directory](https://halal.muis.gov.sg/halal/establishments), [Singapore Food Agency](https://www.sfa.gov.sg/), and current official business sites. The drafts distinguish MUIS-certified premises, Muslim-owned businesses, and certified packaged products. Seasonal offers, menu items, addresses, prices, delivery slots, and certificates should be rechecked immediately before publication.

## Feature-image production prompts

All images were created separately with OpenAI Imagegen, then converted to 1600×900 WebP at quality 88. Every prompt used the common brand direction: *premium but approachable editorial food photography; warm cream stone surface and background; deep teal linen or ceramics; restrained copper details; soft natural daylight; realistic textures; generous clean negative space toward the upper left; Singapore magazine aesthetic; 16:9 landscape; no text, lettering, watermark, logo, people, hands, alcohol, pork, gore, or religious symbols.*

The individual scene prompts were:

1. **Desserts:** A curated platter of pandan cake, macarons, gelato scoops and golden waffles, with a small slice of layered cake and a teal cup in the background.
2. **Mooncakes:** Two traditional baked mooncakes, one cut open to reveal a golden yolk centre, presented on a dark teal plate beside tea and a subtle copper lantern.
3. **Vietnamese food:** A steaming bowl of pho with herbs and lime beside a crisp banh mi, fresh rice-paper rolls and Vietnamese coffee.
4. **Peranakan food:** A refined Peranakan spread with ayam buah keluak, blue pea rice, achar and sambal in patterned teal ceramics with restrained heritage details.
5. **Cake delivery:** A modern celebration cake inside an open unbranded kraft delivery box, with a simple delivery tag, cake server and teal ribbon nearby.
6. **Food delivery:** An appetising family meal in unbranded recyclable delivery boxes—grilled chicken, rice, vegetables and sides—with a teal insulated bag in the background.
7. **Halal meat:** A clean premium butcher still life of marbled beef steaks, lamb chops and chicken on separate pale stone boards, with herbs and pepper; fresh, hygienic and non-graphic.
8. **Snacks:** A generous office and gifting snack spread of crackers, curry puffs, biscuits, nuts and individually wrapped unlabelled treats arranged in open teal boxes.
9. **Ice cream:** Gelato scoops in pistachio, chocolate and mango, a bubble waffle and sealed plain takeaway pints arranged as a cool dessert still life.
10. **Chocolate:** Dark and milk chocolate bars, pralines, truffles, cocoa nibs and a small chocolate cake slice beside an elegant unbranded teal gift box.

## Pre-publication gate

The posts are scheduled, but these checks still apply before the deployment containing them goes live:

1. Recheck every certification and time-sensitive vendor claim against the exact current premises or product.
2. Confirm related draft slugs will publish in a sensible order so internal links do not lead to unavailable pages.
3. Confirm the final editorial approval covers tone, recommendations, feature image, title, and metadata.
4. Deploy the schedule, then monitor indexing and performance after each article goes live.
